About Our College

Shri Shankaracharya Institute of Professional Management & Technology (SSIPMT), Raipur is a premier engineering college in Chhattisgarh. The college provides undergraduate courses in Computer Science, IT, Electronics, Mechanical, and Civil engineering.

Our department has modern computer labs with high-speed internet, experienced faculty, and good placement opportunities.

To provide quality technical education and develop skilled engineers.

Mission

To encourage innovation, practical knowledge, and discipline in students.
